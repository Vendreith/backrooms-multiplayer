## 3. Photosensitivity, Flashing Lights & Audio Warning

> [!WARNING]
> **HEALTH & PHOTOSENSITIVITY NOTICE:**  
> This horror game intentionally includes flickering fluorescent lights, red emergency alarm strobes (`Level !`), CRT/VHS glitch artifacts, screen shake, rapid camera cuts, and sudden loud horror sound effects. Individuals with photosensitive epilepsy or sensitivity to flashing imagery or intense audio should adjust settings (`Graphics`, `CRT/Glitch` sliders, `SFX` volume) or refrain from playing.

## 4. Multiplayer Conduct & Room Safety

- Because Online Multiplayer runs peer-to-peer via WebRTC room codes (`?room=...`), players are responsible for who they invite to their sessions.
- Players agree not to use external scripts or network floods to crash, DoS, or harass other players' browser sessions or abuse public PeerJS/STUN infrastructure.
- Gameplay in Solo or private Co-op sessions is unmoderated; no automated voice chat or persistent text chat server exists in the game.
