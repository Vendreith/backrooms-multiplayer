## 1. Legal Assessment: Thematic Inspiration vs. Derivative Adaptation

Under `https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/licensing-guide` and **CC BY-SA 3.0**:
- Simply building a yellow office maze or surreal pool labyrinth using generic architectural ideas is general thematic inspiration;
- However, because `src/levels/catalog.js`, `src/constants.js`, and in-game HUD overlays directly reuse canonical **Backrooms level numbers & titles** (*"Level 37: The Poolrooms"*, *"Level !: Run For Your Life"*, *"Level 974: Kitty's House"*, *"Level 9223372036854775807: True Final Void"*), canonical **Backrooms entity concepts** (*Smilers*, *Hounds*, *Facelings*, *Skin-Stealers*, *Kitty*, *Hydrolitis*, *The Thing on Level 7*), canonical **items/groups** (*Almond Water*, *M.E.G. Operative*), and adapted descriptions/mechanics derived from specific wiki pages, **the Backrooms-derived content portions of this project constitute adaptations of CC BY-SA 3.0 works**.

**Mandatory CC BY-SA 3.0 Compliance Rules Applied Here:**
1. All adapted Backrooms creative expression in this game is expressly licensed under **CC BY-SA 3.0** (never locked under All Rights Reserved).
2. No DRM, technological copy-lock, or conflicting commercial-prohibition is imposed on the CC BY-SA 3.0 adapted portions.
3. Original authors from Backrooms Wikidot / Fandom are attributed individually below and in the playable **CREDITS & LEGAL** dialog.
4. Architectural transformations made by Vendreith (procedural 3D level layouts, repair-terminal escape loop, multiplayer rescue & crouch-vent mechanics, and Vietnamese localization) are explicitly identified as modifications.
