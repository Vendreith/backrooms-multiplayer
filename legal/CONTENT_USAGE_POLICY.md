# Content Creation, Streaming & Community Usage Policy

**Project:** Backrooms // Level 0 (Found Footage Multiplayer & AI Survival)  
**Developer:** Vendreith (`https://github.com/Vendreith/backrooms-multiplayer`)

---

## 1. Gameplay Videos, Livestreams & Monetization

Content creators, streamers, and players are **explicitly permitted** to:
- Record gameplay videos, Let's Plays, reviews, guides, and clips of **Backrooms // Level 0**;
- Livestream gameplay on platforms such as **YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Facebook Gaming**, and similar services;
- Monetize gameplay videos and livestreams through standard platform advertising, channel memberships, or creator sponsorships;
- Take and publish screenshots or found-footage captures of game levels.

**Audio Notice for Streamers:** While the project itself grants full permission for gameplay footage, third-party music tracks currently flagged as **Category E** in [ASSET_PROVENANCE.md](./ASSET_PROVENANCE.md) may trigger automated third-party Content ID claims until replaced. Muters or Music Volume sliders in the Lobby/Pause menu (`0%`) can be used at any time.

**Attribution Appreciated:** Linking `https://vendreith.github.io/backrooms-multiplayer/` or crediting `Vendreith` (and Backrooms Wiki contributors under CC BY-SA 3.0) in video descriptions is appreciated.

- **Permitted Without Further Permission (Subject to Upstream Licenses):**
  - Reusing and adapting **Backrooms Wiki-derived lore, descriptions, and catalog entries** under **CC BY-SA 3.0** (`https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/`) with proper attribution and ShareAlike;
  - Reusing **ambientCG** textures/decals under **CC0 1.0**;
  - Reusing **Kevin MacLeod** tracks (`ambient_spacial.mp3`, `chase_apprehension.mp3`, `chase_chaos.mp3`) under **CC BY 3.0**;
  - Reusing open-source libraries (`three`, `postprocessing`, `peerjs`, etc.) under `MIT`, `Zlib`, or `BSD-3-Clause`.

  - Extracting, selling, or repackaging **Vendreith's original proprietary code** (`Copyright © 2026 Vendreith. All rights reserved.`) — including custom GLSL shaders (`Preset 5: Soft Upscale / Dreamcam`), procedural level engines, WebRTC netcode, or HUD — as a competing game, paid asset pack, or rebranded clone;
  - Redistributing **Category E** flagged third-party audio or raw `Soldier.glb` model files.
