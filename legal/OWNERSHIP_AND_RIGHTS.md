Copyright law, trademark/branding rights, and collaborative creative licenses operate independently. This document clarifies those boundaries so the project protects legitimate creator rights without overclaiming exclusive ownership over shared community terms.

## 2. Explicit Non-Claims & Anti-Overreach Statement

1. **No False Trademark Registration:** Neither `"Backrooms // Level 0"` nor `"Vendreith"` is a registered trademark (`®`). No USPTO, EUIPO, NOIP, or other trademark registration number is claimed.
2. **No False Copyright Registration:** While original human-authored works receive automatic copyright protection upon fixation in Berne Convention jurisdictions, no formal government copyright registration certificate is claimed.
3. **No Official Affiliation:** This project is **not** affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by `backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com`, `backrooms.fandom.com`, Kane Parsons (Kane Pixels), A24, or Jared Pike.
4. **Severability Consideration for Commercial or Closed-Source Future Releases:** Because `src/levels/catalog.js`, `src/constants.js`, and several level/map files adapt named Backrooms wiki levels and lore, any future decision to sell or closed-source the game would require legal counsel review of CC BY-SA 3.0 derivative boundaries or cleanly separating CC BY-SA 3.0 narrative/data packages from original engine code.
