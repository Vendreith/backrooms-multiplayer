Privacy Summary & Multiplayer Mode (PeerJS / WebRTC)

• The game does not collect account or email information, nor uses tracking/advertising cookies.
• Graphics and volume settings are saved locally on the browser via localStorage (br_gfx_v2, br_fx_v2, br_fps_v2, br_aspect_v2, br_sens_v2, br_mvol_v2, br_svol_v2).
• Online Multiplayer uses WebRTC through PeerJS Cloud (0.peerjs.com) and public STUN servers (Google / Cloudflare / Metered), which exposes player IP addresses to others in the same room. Only share room codes with trusted friends or play in Solo mode.
